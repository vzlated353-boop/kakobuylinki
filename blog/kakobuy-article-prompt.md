## CRITICAL: Website Positioning

- ✅ Organizes product information into browsable categories
- ✅ Curates product listings with pricing references and descriptions
- ✅ Provides shopping guides and category resources
- ✅ Links to external shopping resources (Google Spreadsheet)
- ❌ Does NOT sell products
- ❌ Does NOT process payments or orders
- ❌ Does NOT operate warehouses or inspect products
- ❌ Does NOT handle shipping or logistics
- ❌ Does NOT provide customer support

**All generated content MUST reflect this positioning.**

## Content Requirements

### 1. Keyword Usage
- **Primary keyword**: Must appear in:
  - H1 title
  - First paragraph (within first 100 words)
  - At least one H2 heading
  - Conclusion paragraph
  - Total 3-5 times throughout the article
- **Keyword density**: 1.5%-2.5%
- **LSI keywords**: Include 5-8 related terms naturally

### 2. Data Points (Must Include)
Every article must include at least 3 of these **accurate** data types:
- Product type count: "The directory includes 50+ sneaker styles"
- Price range: "$14.99-$139.99"
- Category breakdown: "Organized into 8 sub-categories"
- Product characteristics: "Available in multiple colorways and materials"
- Style variations: "From classic designs to modern interpretations"

**IMPORTANT**: Do NOT fabricate:
- ❌ Order counts ("410+ orders")
- ❌ Quality pass rates ("94% quality pass rate")
- ❌ Satisfaction rates ("93% buyer satisfaction")
- ❌ Shipping times ("10-14 days to US/EU")
- ❌ Repurchase rates ("72% of buyers reorder")
- ❌ User numbers ("500,000 satisfied buyers")

### 3. Internal Links
- **In-text links**: 2-3 links within the main content
- **Related Guides section**: 3 links at the end
- **Anchor text**: Must include the target page's keyword
- **Link format**: `[Anchor text with keyword](relative-path-to-file.md)`

### 4. E-E-A-T Compliance
- **Experience**: Show understanding of the product category and user needs
- **Expertise**: Explain product characteristics accurately
- **Authoritativeness**: Reference the scope and organization of the directory
- **Trustworthiness**: Acknowledge that prices/availability may change, be transparent about what the directory is

### 5. Tone and Style
- **Professional**: Informative, helpful
- **User-focused**: Address reader needs and research goals
- **Conversational**: Use "you" naturally
- **Clear**: Assertive but not arrogant

### 6. Product Examples
For category/brand pages, include 3-5 specific product types:
```
- **Product Name**: $Price | Key characteristic | Brief description
```

For feature pages, include relevant examples that demonstrate the feature.

### 7. FAQ Questions
Choose from these common question types:
- "What is [keyword]?"
- "What information does [keyword] provide?"
- "How is [keyword] organized?"
- "What price range can I find in [keyword]?"
- "What product types are available in [keyword]?"
- "How do I browse [keyword]?"
- "Where can I find [keyword]?"

### 8. Conclusion Elements
- Summarize 2-3 key points about the category
- Reinforce the value of the organized directory
- Include a clear call to action to browse the spreadsheet
- Mention the spreadsheet one final time

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ **Keyword stuffing**: Don't force keyword unnaturally
- ❌ **Generic content**: Don't write vague statements without substance
- ❌ **Fabricated data**: Never invent order counts, satisfaction rates, or quality pass rates
- ❌ **False service descriptions**: Never describe warehouse, QC, shipping, or customer support
- ❌ **Missing internal links**: Must include 5+ total internal links
- ❌ **Copying existing articles**: All content must be 100% original
- ❌ **Ignoring E-E-A-T**: Must demonstrate experience, expertise, authority, trust
- ❌ **Absolute claims**: Avoid "always," "never," "guaranteed" — use "typically," "usually," "most"
- ❌ **Banned terms**: Check against BANNED_TERMS.md before finalizing
