# How to Order with Kakobuy: From Product Link to Parcel Submission

A useful ordering guide should help you make the next decision, not promise that every order follows the same schedule or costs the same amount. Kakobuy's public homepage describes a workflow in which customers choose products, sellers send items to a China warehouse, warehouse staff check arrivals, and customers later submit an international parcel.[1] Use that flow to track your order stage by stage.

This is an independent explanation of the public workflow. Always rely on the live Kakobuy account, current [Help Center](https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question), and current shipping estimate before submitting an order, payment, or parcel.

## Step 1: Validate the item before submitting an order

Before using a product link, make a record of the information that actually determines what you are asking a seller to provide. Check the product name, variant, colour, size, quantity, seller/store information, and any item-specific note. If the product page is ambiguous, clarify it before ordering; a vague description is difficult to correct once the item has moved through the process.

Also consider whether the item could affect later shipment. The fact that an item can be selected does not establish that it can travel on every international route or be packed with every other item. Destination rules and account prompts should control that decision.

## Step 2: Submit the order and follow the account status

After you submit an order, use the order status in your account as the record of what is happening. Do not build a plan around a generic delivery date: seller dispatch timing, product availability, seller response, and later logistics choices can differ by order.

Kakobuy's public site states that it can help purchase items from different sellers and that those items can arrive at the platform warehouse.[1] That means you may have several orders at different stages at the same time. Keep the product record for each one, particularly if you intend to consider them together later.

## Step 3: Review the warehouse stage

The public workflow says an arriving item is checked and that feedback is provided after warehouse arrival.[1] At this point, your task is to read the status information available in the account and decide whether the item is ready for the next step.

Use this checkpoint to answer four practical questions:

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Has the item reached the relevant warehouse status? | A parcel cannot be planned around an item that has not reached the warehouse stage. |
| Is the item information consistent with the order you recorded? | Resolve a discrepancy while the order is still in the relevant support window. |
| Are you waiting for other items? | Combining or separating parcels is a later decision, not an automatic default. |
| Is there a storage deadline or after-sales condition to consider? | Current storage and return/exchange terms can affect the urgency and available options. |

The official warehouse guidance publishes a normal storage period beginning from the stocked status and reminds users to monitor their own order timing.[2] Refer to the live rule and your account, because exceptions and current conditions can apply.

## Step 4: Decide whether to submit one parcel or wait

Kakobuy's Help Center says that warehouse items from its purchasing and forwarding services can, in ordinary cases, be submitted together or separately. It also says a weight limit or security/customs issue can prevent combination, in which case the system will provide a prompt.[3]

That rule creates a simple decision framework. Submit a parcel only after you have checked the live constraints, not merely because several items appear in storage.

| If your situation is… | Check before choosing |
| --- | --- |
| You want to send one item now | The current destination route, the official quoted amount, and any item-specific restriction. |
| You want to wait for more items | The storage status of the current item and whether later items are likely to be compatible. |
| You want to combine items | Current parcel weight/size limits, security or customs prompts, destination requirements, and account eligibility. |
| You see an exception or restriction | Use the live Help Center or support path before submitting; do not guess based on another person's parcel. |

## Step 5: Submit international shipping using current information

International shipment is a separate transaction from the item order. When your account indicates that items can be submitted, review the route options and any information requested for the destination. Use the official estimate and live account display for current fees, available providers, destination rules, and required information.

Do not treat historical blog posts, screenshots, social-media reports, or another user's quote as a current shipping offer. Public platform announcements and help topics can change, and the correct choice depends on your own parcel and destination.

## If you need to pay or change an order

Before a payment or top-up, follow the **[Kakobuy payment guide](/blog/kakobuy-payment-guide/)**. If an item is already stored and you are deciding what to do next, use the **[Kakobuy warehouse guide](/blog/kakobuy-warehouse-guide/)**. For a broad first-order overview, start with **[Getting started with Kakobuy](/blog/kakobuy-getting-started/)**.

For a return, exchange, cancellation, or a seller-specific issue, the official terms indicate that eligibility depends on factors such as the seller, the item condition, and the order stage. Use the current [return/exchange rule](https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=51) rather than assuming that a request will be accepted.
