## Adidas Tracksuit Sizing Guide

Adidas tracksuits generally fit true to size, but consider:

- **Athletic Fit**: Tiro runs snug, consider size up for layering
- **Classic Fit**: Firebird and SST true to size
- **Relaxed Fit**: Essentials and Adicolor true to size or roomy

**Jacket Sizing**:
- Consider layering needs
- Check sleeve length
- Verify shoulder fit

**Pants Sizing**:
- Check inseam length

## Care and Maintenance

### Washing Instructions
- Turn inside out before washing
- Use cold water to preserve colors
- Avoid bleach and fabric softeners
- Tumble dry low or hang dry

### Storage Tips
- Hang jackets to maintain shape
- Fold pants neatly or hang
- Store in cool, dry environment
- Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading

### General Maintenance
- Treat stains immediately
- Avoid excessive washing to preserve prints
- Check zippers and hardware regularly
- Follow care label instructions
