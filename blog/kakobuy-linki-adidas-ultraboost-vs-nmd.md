## Silhouette Overview

Both the Ultraboost and NMD showcase Adidas's Boost technology, but serve different purposes in the sneaker landscape.

### Adidas Ultraboost: The Performance Runner

Released in 2015, the Ultraboost represents Adidas's premium running technology:

- **Full-length Boost midsole** with thousands of TPU pellets
- **Primeknit upper** that adapts to foot shape
- **Continental rubber outsole** for superior grip
- **Torsion System** for midfoot stability
- **Performance-oriented design** for running and athletic use

### Adidas NMD: The Lifestyle Innovator

Introduced in 2015 as well, the NMD (Nomad) blends retro running aesthetics with modern lifestyle design:

- **Boost midsole plugs** visible on lateral and medial sides
- **Primeknit or mesh upper** for comfortable fit
- **Minimalist silhouette** with clean lines
- **Retro running inspiration** with modern materials
- **Lifestyle-focused design** for casual wear
