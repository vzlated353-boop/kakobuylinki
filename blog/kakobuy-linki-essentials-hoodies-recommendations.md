## Care and Maintenance

### Washing Instructions
- Turn inside out before washing
- Use cold water to prevent shrinking
- Avoid bleach and harsh detergents
- Tumble dry low or hang dry

### Storage Tips
- Fold rather than hang to prevent stretching
- Store in cool, dry environment
- Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading
- Use cedar blocks for freshness

### General Maintenance
- Treat stains immediately
- Avoid excessive washing to preserve fabrics
- Use fabric softener sparingly
- Check care labels for specific instructions
