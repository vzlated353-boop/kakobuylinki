## FAQ

**Q: Which is more versatile?**

A: T-shirts are more versatile as base layers that work in all seasons and contexts. Hoodies are versatile as standalone pieces and layers but are more seasonal.

**Q: Which offers better value?**

A: T-shirts offer better value as affordable entry points into brands. Hoodies cost more but provide more material and versatility for the price.

**Q: Can I wear hoodies in summer?**

A: Lightweight hoodies work in summer evenings or air-conditioned environments. For hot weather, t-shirts are more comfortable and practical.

**Q: How many of each should I own?**

A: Start with 3-5 essential t-shirts and 2-3 hoodies. Expand based on your style, climate, and wardrobe needs. Quality over quantity is key.
