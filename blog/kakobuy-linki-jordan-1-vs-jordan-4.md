## Silhouette Overview

The Jordan 1 and Jordan 4 represent two distinct eras of Michael Jordan's signature shoe line, each with unique design elements and cultural significance.

### Jordan 1: The Original

Released in 1985, the Jordan 1 established the foundation for the entire Jordan line. Designed by Peter Moore, this silhouette features:

- **High-top construction** with ankle support
- **Simple panel design** with clean lines
- **Nike Air branding** on the tongue (original versions)
- **Wings logo** on the ankle collar
- **Minimalist aesthetic** that works with various outfits

### Jordan 4: The Evolution

Introduced in 1989 and designed by Tinker Hatfield, the Jordan 4 brought innovation and complexity:

- **Visible Air unit** in the heel for cushioning
- **Plastic wing eyelets** for lace support
- **Mesh panels** on the quarter and tongue
- **Distinctive heel tab** with Jumpman logo
- **More aggressive styling** with multiple material layers
