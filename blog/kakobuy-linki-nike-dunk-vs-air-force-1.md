## Silhouette Overview

Both the Nike Dunk and Air Force 1 represent cornerstones of sneaker culture, each with distinct heritage and design philosophy.

### Nike Dunk: The Skate Heritage

Originally released in 1985 as a basketball shoe, the Dunk found its true calling in skate culture:

- **Low-profile design** with clean lines
- **Padded collar and tongue** for skateboarding protection
- **Simple panel construction** with minimal overlays
- **Flat outsole** optimized for board feel
- **Versatile colorways** that work with various styles

### Air Force 1: The Classic

Released in 1982, the Air Force 1 pioneered visible Air cushioning:

- **High-top origins** with low-top versions now most popular
- **Visible Air unit** in the sole for cushioning
- **Distinctive perforations** on the toe box
- **Metallic tag** on the laces (AF1 Low '07)
- **Bold, substantial presence** on foot

## FAQ

**Q: Which shoe is more comfortable for all-day wear?**

A: The Air Force 1 generally offers better all-day comfort due to the visible Air unit and plush cushioning. However, some users prefer the lighter feel of the Dunk for active days.

**Q: Can I wear either shoe for sports or exercise?**

A: While both originated as basketball shoes, they're primarily lifestyle sneakers today. Neither is recommended for serious athletic performance by modern standards.

**Q: Which shoe is easier to keep clean?**

A: Both are relatively easy to clean, but Air Force 1 (especially white colorways) shows dirt more readily. Dunk versions in darker colors or suede may require more specialized care.
