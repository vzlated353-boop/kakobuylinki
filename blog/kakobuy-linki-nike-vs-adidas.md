## Brand Overview

Nike and Adidas represent two pillars of athletic footwear and apparel, each with distinct design philosophies and target audiences.

**Nike** emphasizes innovation and performance technology, featuring iconic designs like the Air Force 1, Air Max series, and Dunk. The brand focuses on bold aesthetics and cutting-edge cushioning systems.

**Adidas** balances heritage styles with modern comfort, known for the Three-Stripe design language, Boost cushioning technology, and classic silhouettes like the Superstar, Stan Smith, and Ultraboost.

## Sizing Differences

Understanding sizing variations is crucial when researching products through Kakobuy Linki.

### Nike Sizing Characteristics

- **General Fit**: True to size for most foot shapes
- **Exceptions**: 
  - Nike Dunk runs slightly narrow in the toe box
  - Air Force 1 runs about half a size large
  - Jordan models (produced by Nike) have specific fit patterns

### Adidas Sizing Characteristics

- **General Fit**: Uses European sizing as baseline
- **Notable Patterns**:
  - Yeezy Boost 350 V2 tends to run small (size up half to full size)
  - Samba and Gazelle run slightly narrow
  - Ultraboost fits snugly through midfoot

## Material and Technology Comparison

### Nike Technology

- **Air Cushioning**: Visible Air units in Air Max series provide responsive cushioning
- **Primeknit**: Used in select models for adaptive fit
- **Leather Quality**: Premium leather uppers in classic silhouettes
- **Design Focus**: Bold visual statements and performance innovation

### Adidas Technology

- **Boost Cushioning**: TPU pellet-based midsole offering energy return
- **Primeknit**: Adaptive knit upper technology (similar to Nike's Flyknit)
- **Continental Rubber**: Premium outsole material for enhanced grip
- **Design Focus**: Heritage aesthetics merged with modern comfort
