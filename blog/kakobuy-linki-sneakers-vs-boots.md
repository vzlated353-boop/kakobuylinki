## FAQ

**Q: Which is more comfortable for all-day wear?**

A: Modern sneakers generally offer better cushioning and comfort for extended wear. Boots provide support but require break-in periods and are heavier.

**Q: Which offers better value long-term?**

A: Quality boots offer better long-term value through durability and repairability. Sneakers are cheaper initially but need replacement more frequently.

**Q: Can I wear sneakers in winter?**

A: Some sneakers work in mild winter conditions, but boots provide better protection from cold, wet, and snowy conditions.

**Q: Which is more versatile for styling?**

A: Sneakers offer more versatility across casual contexts. Boots are versatile in smart-casual and rugged contexts but less suitable for athletic wear.
