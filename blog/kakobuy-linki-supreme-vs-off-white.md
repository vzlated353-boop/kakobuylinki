## FAQ

**Q: Which brand is more exclusive?**

A: Both are exclusive in different ways. Supreme uses drop model scarcity. Off-White uses luxury pricing and limited distribution. Both maintain high exclusivity through different methods.

**Q: Is Off-White worth the luxury pricing?**

A: Value depends on your priorities. Off-White offers luxury materials and high-fashion design. If you appreciate these aspects and can afford it, the premium may be worthwhile. Supreme offers street credibility at lower prices.

**Q: Which brand has better resale value?**

A: Supreme limited drops often appreciate significantly. Off-White maintains luxury resale value. Both hold value well, but Supreme's drop model can create higher peaks for rare pieces.

**Q: Can I wear both brands together?**

A: Yes, but carefully. Both work in streetwear contexts but have different aesthetics. Use one as the focal point and the other as complementary. Balance is key to avoiding logo overload.
