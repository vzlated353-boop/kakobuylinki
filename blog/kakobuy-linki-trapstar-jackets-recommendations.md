### Puffer Jackets

- Use gentle cycle or professional cleaning

- Store properly to maintain loft

### Bomber and Varsity Jackets
- Spot clean when possible
- Professional cleaning recommended
- Avoid excessive washing
- Store on proper hangers

### Windbreakers
