## Silhouette Overview

Both Yeezy 350 and Yeezy 500 represent Kanye West's collaboration with Adidas, but feature distinct design approaches and technology implementations.

### Yeezy Boost 350: The Iconic Runner

Released in 2015, the Yeezy 350 revolutionized sneaker design:

- **Full-length Boost midsole** for responsive cushioning
- **Primeknit upper** with distinctive stripe pattern
- **Low-profile silhouette** with sleek lines
- **Semi-translucent outsole** (V2 version)
- **Pull tab** at heel for easy on/off

### Yeezy 500: The Retro-Futurist

Introduced in 2018, the Yeezy 500 takes a different approach:

- **Adiprene cushioning** (not Boost) in the heel
- **Mesh and suede upper** with multiple panels
- **Chunky, retro-inspired silhouette**
- **Gum rubber outsole** for durability
- **No laces design** (slip-on style)

## FAQ

**Q: Which Yeezy is more comfortable?**

A: Yeezy 350 offers superior comfort with full-length Boost cushioning. The 500 provides decent comfort with Adiprene but doesn't match the 350's premium feel.

**Q: How much should I size up for Yeezy 350?**

A: Most users need to size up half to full a size from their normal size. If you have wide feet, consider a full size up. Always consult the sizing guide.

**Q: Can I wear Yeezy 500 for sports?**

A: Neither Yeezy is designed for serious athletic performance. The 500 offers more structure but lacks the responsive cushioning needed for sports. Both are lifestyle sneakers.

**Q: Which Yeezy holds value better?**

A: Yeezy 350 generally holds value better due to higher demand and iconic status. Limited colorways of both can appreciate, but 350 is more sought after.
