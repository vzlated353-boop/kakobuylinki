## Care and Maintenance

### Primeknit Uppers (350 V2)
- Use soft brush for cleaning
- Avoid excessive water exposure
- Use specialized sneaker cleaner
- Air dry naturally

### Suede and Mesh (500, 700)
- Use suede brush for maintenance
- Apply protective spray
- Avoid wet conditions
- Store in dry environment

### EVA Foam (Slide, Foam Runner)
- Wipe clean with damp cloth
- Avoid harsh chemicals
- Air dry completely
- Store away from direct sunlight

### General Tips
- Rotate between multiple pairs
- Use shoe trees to maintain shape
- Clean stains immediately
- Store in cool, dry environment
