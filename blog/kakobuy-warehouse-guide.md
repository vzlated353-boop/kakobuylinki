# Kakobuy Warehouse Guide: Storage, Item Review and Parcel Decisions

The warehouse stage is where an item order turns into a shipping decision. Kakobuy's public homepage says that items from different sellers can arrive at its China warehouse, where arriving goods are checked and feedback is provided before the customer submits an international parcel.[1] A warehouse guide should therefore focus on status, timing, item compatibility, and current route information—not on generic product recommendations.

This is an independent guide based on public Kakobuy information. Storage terms, parcel restrictions, available routes, fees, and after-sales rules can change. Check the live [Help Center](https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question) and your own account before you submit a parcel or request a service.

## What changes when an item reaches the warehouse

An item shown as ordered is not necessarily ready for international shipment. The public workflow separates seller delivery to the warehouse, warehouse inspection/feedback, and later parcel submission.[1] Your account status is the operational source for the item in front of you.

At warehouse arrival, take a deliberate pause. Compare the available item information with the record you made when ordering, then decide whether you need to act now or wait for other orders. If something appears inconsistent, use the platform's current support and after-sales process while the item is in the relevant status.

## Track the storage window—not an assumed shipping date

Kakobuy's public Help Center says normal purchasing and forwarding parcels are stored for **100 days** from the order status that indicates the item is stocked. The same page says a reminder is sent after 90 days and that goods without a submitted parcel or feedback after the stated period may be treated as abandoned. It also describes a shorter 60-day period for certain unmatched or exceptional parcels from their signed-for status.[2]

The practical rule is simple: read your own status and the live policy, then set a reminder well before the platform's stated deadline. Do not use a generic storage figure as a promise that applies to every order, perishable item, or exceptional parcel. The official page specifically notes that item shelf life is separate from storage handling.[2]

| Warehouse checkpoint | What to review | Safe next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item has not reached warehouse | Seller/order status and any available update | Wait for the correct status rather than planning a parcel around an unarrived item. |
| Item is available in warehouse | Item/status information and any visible issue | Decide whether to keep, pursue the applicable after-sales path, or wait for other items. |
| Storage time is progressing | Current account deadline and official rule | Set a reminder; plan the parcel before the deadline rather than relying on a blog estimate. |
| Parcel is ready to submit | Live route options, destination prompts, package constraints, and quoted amount | Make the shipping decision with current account data. |

## Should you combine items or send them separately?

Kakobuy's public Help Center says purchasing and forwarding items that are available for parcel submission can usually be sent together or separately. The same public answer cautions that weight limits, security requirements, or customs-policy issues can prevent a combination; in those cases, the system may present a prompt.[3]

That means consolidation is a **review step**, not a guaranteed benefit. Before combining items, use the live account to check each of the following.

1. **Eligibility:** Are all items actually shown as ready for parcel submission?
2. **Compatibility:** Could any item trigger a restriction, safety issue, or destination-specific rule?
3. **Parcel constraint:** Does the current system accept the expected weight, dimensions, and item mix?
4. **Destination requirement:** Are you being asked for information that applies to the destination or chosen route?
5. **Live cost:** Does the current estimate still fit the budget you set before ordering?

If the platform shows an incompatibility or a required action, do not override it based on an anecdote from another order. Review the official rule and the current support route for your own parcel.

## Warehouse checks and after-sales decisions

The warehouse stage can also be the point at which a buyer considers return or exchange options. Kakobuy's public after-sales page describes eligibility as conditional: it depends on the seller's terms, product category and condition, the storage/stocked timing, and the then-current rules.[4] It also identifies categories and situations that may be ineligible.

For that reason, use a warehouse guide to organize the decision, not to claim a guaranteed refund or exchange. If you are considering an after-sales request, first check the item status, the current official rule, and any condition the seller/platform presents in the account. Keep the evidence connected to the order rather than relying on a generic price or deadline quoted elsewhere.

## A warehouse-to-parcel checklist

Before you submit international shipping, verify that you can answer each question below from your live account or official source.

- Which items are actually available and eligible to be submitted?
- Does the current warehouse timing require action soon?
- Are you sending now or waiting for another order, and why?
- Does the planned combination meet all live prompts and restrictions?
- Have you checked the current route, destination information, and quoted charge?
- If there is an item issue, have you reviewed the applicable official after-sales path before shipping?
